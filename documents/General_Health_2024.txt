General Health and Hygiene 2024 - Public Advisory

Part 1: Everyday Health and Wellness
1. Maintain a balanced diet rich in proteins and green leafy vegetables for optimal immunity.
2. Adults should consume at least 2 liters of water daily, adjusted for climate 1.
3. Diarrhea management: Start ORS immediately. Zinc supplementation: 20 mg per day for 14 days.
4. Hand hygiene: Wash hands with soap for at least 20 seconds before meals.
5. Sleep guidelines: Ensure 7-8 hours of continuous sleep to improve cognitive function.
6. Physical activity: Engage in at least 150 minutes of moderate aerobic exercise per week.
7. Limit added sugar intake to less than 10% of total daily calories (approx 50 grams).
8. Avoid stagnant water in residential areas to prevent mosquito breeding in sector 1.
9. Mental health check 1: Practice mindfulness or meditation to reduce stress levels.
