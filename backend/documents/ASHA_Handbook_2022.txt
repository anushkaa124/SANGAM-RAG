ASHA Handbook 2022 - Guidelines for Community Health Workers

Chapter 1: Community Health Initiatives
1. ASHA workers must conduct routine visits in sector 1.
2. Maternal health tracking involves 4 visits per month for pregnant women in zone 1.
3. Newborn care requires checking temperature and weight on days 1, 3, 7, 14, 21, and 28.
4. Immunization drives in block 1 must record OPV, BCG, and Pentavalent doses.
5. Nutritional counseling: Encourage breastfeeding exclusively for the first 6 months.
6. Iron and Folic Acid (IFA) supplementation: Provide 180 IFA tablets to pregnant women.
7. Record keeping for malaria and dengue cases in register book 1.
8. Follow-up on severely malnourished children, referring them to the nearest NRC.
9. Conduct monthly meetings with the Village Health Sanitation and Nutrition Committee (VHSNC).
