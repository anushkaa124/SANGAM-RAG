National Health Mission (NHM) Guidelines 2024

Section 1: Rural Health Infrastructure and Policy
1. Sub-health centers in district 1 should cater to a population of 5000 in plain areas.
2. Each center must be staffed by at least one ANM and one male health worker.
3. Essential medicine kits for block 1 must be restocked quarterly.
4. Iron and Folic Acid (IFA) update 1: Provide 180 IFA tablets to combat severe anemia.
5. Tuberculosis (TB) DOTS providers will receive an incentive of Rs. 1000 per cured patient.
6. Non-Communicable Disease (NCD) screening for hypertension and diabetes for age > 30.
7. Digital health records update 1: Ensure all patient data is synced to the e-Sanjeevani portal.
8. Ambulance response time for emergency maternal care must be under 30 minutes.
9. Financial allocation for sanitation improvements in region 1 increased by 15%.
